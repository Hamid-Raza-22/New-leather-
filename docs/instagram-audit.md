**Target profile:** https://www.instagram.com/new_leathersrl/ (`@new_leathersrl`)

the generic login wall rather than profile content, and no attempt was made to
bypass the login, CAPTCHA or any other access control. The audit therefore
relies on public business directories and client-supplied information.

> none are hard-coded anywhere in the website.

## 3. Public company record

Business directories consistently list a company matching the client-supplied
address:

| Field | Value | Source type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal name | NEW LEATHER S.R.L. | Italian business directories |
| Registered address | Via Veregrense 145, 63812 Montegranaro (FM), Marche, Italy | Italian business directories; matches client-supplied address |
| Sector (registry) | Intermediation / wholesale of raw and semi-finished textile materials and raw hides | Italian business directories |
| Sector (trade directory) | Leather goods / leather cases, clothing & accessories | Europages listing |

Important caveats:

- These are third-party directory listings, not statements from the company.
  They are **not** reproduced as claims on the website.
- Registry activity codes and trade-directory categories disagree with each
  other about what the company does. Because of that, the website deliberately
  avoids saying whether New Leather supplies, represents, or manufactures.
- Other unrelated companies share the name "New Leather" (at least one in
  Solofra, one in Castel Volturno, and "New Leather Tecnology"). None of their
  information has been used.
- Founding date, revenue, headcount and similar figures appear in directories
  but are **not** used anywhere: they are unverified third-party data and the
  brief forbids fabricated or unconfirmed factual claims.

## 5. Content themes

The website's section structure follows the themes requested in the brief
(material, quality, research, experience, applications, team, events). These are
**structural** decisions, not observations of the feed. Until imagery and
captions are supplied, no theme is presented as a factual description of the
company's Instagram output.

## 6. Collections — VALDARNO

Investigated as requested. Public sources associate the name "Valdarno" in a
leather context with **I.P. Valdarno International S.p.A.**, a separate tannery
based in San Miniato / Ponte a Egola, Tuscany, founded 1996, with its own brand
("Fiesole") and its own certifications.

**No public evidence links VALDARNO to New Leather srl.** Ownership, licensing,
distribution or partnership is therefore **not** claimed anywhere on the site.
The Collections page states this explicitly and marks it
`[CLIENT CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]`.
